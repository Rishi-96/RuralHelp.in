## Brand & Style

This design system translates the dignity, industrious craft, and communal trust of regional Indian commerce into a modern digital platform. It rejects ephemeral tech tropes, hyper-slick silicon minimalism, and sterile SaaS surfaces in favor of an **Authentic Editorial Commerce** aesthetic—tactile, grounded, trustworthy, and culturally rooted.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Rooted & Trustworthy:** Built on solid forest tones and unbleached heritage papers, projecting institutional stability, financial security, and long-term partnership.
- **Industrious & Dignified:** Celebrates local retail, micro-enterprises, and artisanal supply chains with rigorous craft, structured layouts, and editorial respect.
- **Vital & Forward-Looking:** Powered by an electric agricultural lime accent that symbolizes growth, modern digital tools, and active vitality without compromising heritage.

### Visual Style Philosophy
- **Structured Architectural Grid:** Rigorous lines, hairline frame dividers, and distinct content compartments reminiscent of vintage ledgers, passbooks, and modern architectural journals.
- **Tactile Paper Surfaces:** Warm ivory canvases replace synthetic pure whites, grounding data density in warm physical space.
- **Physical Authenticity:** Imagery uses crisp archival framing with fine structural borders. Pure digital dropshadows and diffuse neon gradients are strictly prohibited.

## Colors

The palette balances historical botanical depth with digital utilitarian clarity.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1B4D3E` Deep Evergreen / `#0E382B` Forest Night):** Anchors navigation, top-level branding, critical headings, and primary transactional actions. Evokes security, agricultural abundance, and enduring value.
- **Secondary (`#84CC16` Vital Lime / `#A3E635` Sprout Accent):** High-energy indicator used sparingly for dynamic metrics, positive order states, active notifications, and key conversion signals.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706` Warm Ochre / Brass Amber):** Rooted in traditional brass, marigolds, and market ledger highlights. Applied to pending statuses, warning badges, and verified merchant seals.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B` Slate / `#0F172A` Deep Ink):** Provides razor-sharp legible text rendering, hairline borders, and structural framing.
- **Canvas & Surface Tier:**
  - `surface-canvas`: `#FAF8F5` (Heritage Warm Ivory)
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Crisp Sheet)
  - `surface-subtle`: `#F4EFEA` (Pressed Paper Tint)
  - `border-hairline`: `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.12)` (Ledger Rule)

### Usage Rules
- Avoid stark `#000000` blacks or cold `#F8FAFC` blue-tinted whites. All base surfaces must carry the warm yellow-sand undertone of `#FAF8F5`.
- Do not mix lime accents with amber accents in the same UI component; keep state signaling distinct and purposeful.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the literary warmth and intellectual gravitas of **Newsreader** with the structural clarity and high-density legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

### Font Hierarchy & Roles
- **Headlines (Newsreader):** Used for section titles, merchant narratives, and primary ledger summaries. It infuses the UI with editorial authority and warmth. Headline titles above 32px compress line heights to maintain tight graphic presence.
- **Body & Data (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Calibrated for exceptional multi-column reading, inventory listings, financial tallies, and forms across varying screen resolutions.
- **Labels & Micro-copy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Employs medium-to-bold weights with slight positive tracking (`0.02em` to `0.08em`) to guarantee quick visual scanning for operational shopkeepers under varying ambient lighting.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored in an editorial grid that favors distinct borders, structural columns, and intentional content compartments over arbitrary floating cards.

### Layout Principles
- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop displays standard 12-column layouts; tablets scale to 8 columns; mobile reflows to a structured 4-column system.
- **Ledger-Style Alignment:** Content is grouped via hairline horizontal and vertical dividers. Dense numeric and business metrics sit against solid perimeter margins rather than floating unanchored.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** Deep side margins (`margin-desktop: 3rem`) with fixed sidebar navigation and high-density two- or three-pane workspaces.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Fluid columns with standard `margin: 2rem`, collapsable drawer controls, and side-by-side transaction listings.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** Tight, compact structure with `margin-mobile: 1rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`, prioritizing finger-tap accessibility without inflating card height.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids soft, blurred, floating drop-shadows typical of generic SaaS software. Instead, it creates depth and physical hierarchy using **Tonal Layers**, **Structural Borders**, and **Micro-Offset Inks**.

### Elevation Strategies
- **Primary Depth via Tiers:** Depth is communicated by stacking tonal surfaces:
  - Base canvas sits at `#FAF8F5`.
  - Nested container panels sit at `#FFFFFF`.
  - Inset utility rows and header blocks use `#F4EFEA`.
- **Low-Contrast Structural Outlines:** Every container, card, and operational module is framed by a 1px border (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.10)` to `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.16)`).
- **Physical Offset (Tactile Action):** For interactive buttons or raised utility cards, elevation is achieved via a crisp, unblurred shadow offset: `box-shadow: 2px 2px 0px #0E382B`. This replicates the tactile click of physical shopkeeping tools.
- **Glass & Blur Prohibition:** Frosted glassmorphism and multi-color gradient blurs are not permitted.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft** shape language (`roundedness: 1`). Corners are subtly eased rather than rounded or pill-shaped, conveying industrial durability and clean bookbinding edges.

### Radius Scale
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Buttons, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`). Maintains crisp, precise perimeter edges.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg`):** `8px` (`0.5rem`). Gently distinguishes modular ledger units from background paper surfaces.
- **Large Panels & Modals (`rounded-xl`):** `12px` (`0.75rem`). Reserved for full drawers, slide-overs, and dialogue windows.
- **Pills are strictly prohibited** for standard CTA buttons to preserve an architectural, business-grade footprint. Circular geometry is allowed exclusively for standalone iconography and user avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1B4D3E` (Deep Evergreen) fill with crisp `#FFFFFF` text. Micro-offset border shadow (`1px 1px 0px #0E382B`) on standard state, shifting to `0px 0px` on active press. Padding: `10px 20px`. Border-radius: `4px`.
- **Secondary / Action Accent:** Solid `#84CC16` fill with deep charcoal `#0F172A` text. Used for immediate operational triggers like "Confirm Order" or "Receive Payment".
- **Outline / Ghost:** Transparent background with 1.5px solid `#1B4D3E` border, text `#1B4D3E`. On hover, fills with `#F4EFEA`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Flat layout with 1px border.
  - *Active / Success:* Background `#F7FEE7`, text `#3F6212`, border `rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.4)`.
  - *Pending / Escrow:* Background `#FFFBEB`, text `#B45309`, border `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.3)`.
  - *Archived / Neutral:* Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.15)`.
- Corner radius: `4px`. Typography: `label-md` in uppercase.

### Cards & Ledger Blocks
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.12)`.
- Internal sections are separated by continuous 1px rules rather than independent floating sub-cards.
- Card headers carry a distinct tinted banner (`#F4EFEA`) with `title-sm` or `title-md` font pairings.

### Input Fields
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: 1px solid `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.25)`.
- Height: `44px` for optimal physical touch accuracy.
- Focus State: 2px solid `#1B4D3E` border outline with no diffuse neon glow rings.
- Label: Placed outside and above the field in `label-lg`, colored in `#1E293B`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes use square geometry with `2px` corner radius; Radios are strictly circular.
- Border: 1.5px solid `#1B4D3E`.
- Selected State: Solid `#1B4D3E` background with white check mark icon.

### Imagery & Photography Frames
- Every merchant photo or product thumbnail is encased in a 1px solid border (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.15)`) with an internal `2px` inset white matting line, celebrating local store owners and authentic craft with museum-grade care.
